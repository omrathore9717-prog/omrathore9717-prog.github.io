Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about SIPs, mutual funds and investing.

What is SIP?

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount periodically into mutual funds, helping build wealth through rupee-cost averaging.

Why invest in mutual funds?

Mutual funds offer diversification, professional management and easy access to different asset classes, ideal for both new and experienced investors.

How does SIP work?

With SIP you invest a fixed sum monthly or quarterly; units are purchased at the prevailing NAV, averaging your purchase cost over time.
