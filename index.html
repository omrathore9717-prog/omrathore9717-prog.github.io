Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about SIPs, mutual funds and insurance.

SIP lets you invest a fixed amount regularly into mutual funds, allowing rupee-cost averaging and disciplined savings.

Mutual funds pool investor money and allocate it across stocks, bonds or other assets, managed by professionals to meet investment goals.

ELSS is an equity-linked savings scheme with tax benefits under section 80C, designed for long-term wealth creation through equities.

SIP offers convenience, rupee-cost averaging, and the benefit of disciplined investing over time, reducing the impact of market volatility.

Insurance protects you and your family from financial shocks by covering medical, life and liability expenses when unexpected events occur.
